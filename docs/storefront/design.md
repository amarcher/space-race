# Space Race storefront

The store should feel like the game before it asks for a shipping address.

Palette: deep-space navy #07071a, cockpit blue #151d38, solar gold #ffd93d,
cloud white #f5f2e9, lavender grey #b8bdd2, shuttle orange #ffac69.
Typography: the product's own Unbounded 700/900 for short display headlines;
Inter 400/600 for reading and controls, using the existing self-hosted fonts.

Layout: left-aligned invitation beside a large fan of actual printed cards,
then a portrait campaign film alongside the family-game story, an interactive
Black Hole / Rescue Shuttle example, and real product photography alongside
an explicit pre-order offer. A compact mobile order bar links to that offer.

    brand                       film / how to play / get the game
    A thousand light-years.     real card fan against shuttle artwork
    One more game.              watch the film
    2–4 players                 15–30 minutes       107 cards
    portrait film               the family comeback story
    your move: Black Hole       play Rescue Shuttle / see the escape
    real product photos         price / quantity / shipping / checkout
    shipping and returns        questions / play online

Review against brief: a generic looping space background would hide the actual
product and waste mobile bandwidth. Use the authentic cards as the memorable
hero, then give the existing Higgsfield film its own uncropped portrait stage.
The short interactive example must explain the real Slingshot rule and link to
the actual game. Keep checkout in the same visual world with a product summary,
while preserving the repaired Stripe shipping and confirmation logic.

Media: use latest v11 Rainbows cut from the campaign receipt. No generation
jobs, music changes, or edits to the original campaign. This remains a local
review build; campaign final-mix review and release conditions still apply.
